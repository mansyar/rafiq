# Product Guidelines — Rafiq

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** Rafiq (رفيق) — "companion"
- **Brand line:** "Rafiq — Muslim Companion" (EN) · "Rafiq — Sahabat Muslim" (ID)
- **Positioning:** Trustworthy, private, beautiful, locally-run companion for
  daily Islamic practice.

## Visual Style
- **Direction:** Modern-traditional hybrid — Islamic geometric motifs and
  calligraphic accents executed with clean, modern layouts.
- **Palette:** Gold + emerald on deep neutral/ink backgrounds; restrained use;
  high contrast reserved for sacred text.
- **Motifs:** Geometric patterns (subtle backgrounds, section dividers),
  calligraphic accents for Arabic elements (Bismillah, app marks).
- **Typography:** A strong serif + Arabic-friendly pairing; Arabic rendered with
  proper RTL shaping and high legibility.
- **Sacred text presentation:** Quranic Arabic is always high-contrast and
  unobstructed by decorative elements. No emojis adjacent to Quranic text.

## Voice & Tone
- **Voice:** Warm & reverent — dignified, kind; respects sacred content;
  encouraging without being preachy.
- **Tone guidance:**
  - Do: gentle affirmation, clear functional guidance, calm error messages.
  - Don't: hype language, guilt-inducing reminders, gamified pressure
    ("You're on fire!"), casual slang around worship.
- **Language:** English + Indonesian UI; Arabic for sacred terms (surah names,
  du'a, etc.). Terminology kept consistent across locales.

## UX Principles
1. **Simplicity-first** — a few focused screens (Today, Quran, Log) with a clear
   daily path; every action reachable in one click.
2. **Respect the ritual** — notifications are timely and non-intrusive; adhan
   offers optional full audio.
3. **Trustworthy defaults** — conservative, standard choices: MWL prayer method,
   Umm al-Qura Hijri basis.
4. **Offline-first by default** — local cache, no spinners for bundled content;
   downloads are explicit (audio).
5. **Accessibility** — readable contrast, keyboard navigation, scalable text,
   proper ARIA labels in the web UI.
6. **Consistent translation** — EN/ID terminology managed in one i18n catalog.

## What We Won't Do
- No dark patterns, no data collection, no ads, no premium gates.
- No decorative clutter in the mushaf view — the Quran text is sacred and
  presented plainly.
