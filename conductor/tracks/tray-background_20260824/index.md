## Overview

Makes Rafiq a true always-on companion: close-to-tray with a one-time
explainer hint keeps adhan reminders alive after the window closes; a system
tray icon (monochrome template glyph) exposes a next-prayer info row, show,
and quit; single-instance launch restores a hidden window; and a new Settings
toggle enables launch-at-login via the already-integrated autostart plugin.
